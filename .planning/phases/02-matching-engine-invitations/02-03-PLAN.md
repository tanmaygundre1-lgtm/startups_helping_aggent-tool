<interfaces>
V1 policy: exactly one Team document per Idea (`unique ideaId`); a founder chooses when to form the team and needs at least one accepted invitation, not every required role. Candidates may participate in teams for different ideas; same-idea duplication is prohibited by the Team unique key and accepted invitation/team linkage.
Team fields are `ideaId`, `founderId`, `name`, `members: [{ userId, role, invitationId, joinedAt }]`, `status: Active|Archived`, and timestamps. Store only references and safe read projections.
Invitation statuses are `Pending|Accepted|Declined|Withdrawn`; only Accepted invitations are copied into members. Add/set `teamId` on linked invitations inside the same transaction.
Mongoose 9 transactions require a replica set/sharded deployment. Use `mongoose.connection.transaction()` or an explicit session; pass the same session to every read/write and do not use `Promise.all` inside the transaction. Translate duplicate-key races to 409.
</interfaces>
</context>

| Accepted invitations -> team membership | Multiple documents must become consistent atomically.         |
